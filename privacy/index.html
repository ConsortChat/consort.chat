Privacy

Consort is built to collect no more data than it needs.

The apps

Each Consort app has its own privacy policy.

  • Mobile app privacy policy (draft)
  • Desktop app privacy policy: coming later

Consort servers

The apps connect to a Consort server, and whoever runs that server decides what happens to the data stored on it, including your account, messages and files. Ask them for their privacy policy. If you run your own server, that’s you.

This website

consort.chat uses no cookies, analytics, tracking, or resources from other websites. The server that hosts it may keep ordinary access logs, such as your IP address and the pages you request.

Links to app stores and GitHub take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Consort is open source under the Apache License 2.0. Source code: server, desktop, mobile.
