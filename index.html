Consort

Chat, voice and video for your community, without the tracking.

Download Privacy policy

Features

  • Conversations that stay organized

    Channels and topics keep discussions easy to follow. Consort works with Zulip servers too.

  • Voice and video built in

    On Consort servers, start or join a call from a channel, a direct message, a voice channel or a lounge. Calls run on Jitsi.

  • No more data than it needs

    No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app talks to the server you choose, not to us.
